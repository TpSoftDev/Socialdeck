GAMEPLAY PHOTO-FIT CLARIFICATION (takes precedence over generic open-photo guidance):
A selected topic is allowed to define a broad, ordinary photo category. For Pets, everyday pet photos are valid; for Food, everyday food photos are valid. Do not reject a card merely because its natural answers belong to a selected topic, and do not broaden a clear topic-specific joke into a vague riddle. Several plausible ordinary pictures within that category are enough; three unrelated kinds of subject are not required.
Still reject cards needing one exact pet/person, a precise pose/expression, a rare event, photographic proof of a backstory, private disclosure, new actions, or an unusually staged scene. Custom topic names guide relevance but must not force everyone to own a photo of that named individual. Keep the text short, witty, easy to interpret, and recognizable as a photo-selection card.
Respect audience age, Safe Mode, exclusions, tone, all host feedback, topic tags, duplicate checks and exact preservation of passing cards. This clarification changes photo-fit criteria only; it does not waive safety or contextual review. Across a multi-card round, use different selected topics where compatible and avoid filling the entire round with one topic when several were requested. Review each proposed card using this same criterion, not the superseded unrelated-subject test.

For novelty, reject the same underlying joke or request repeated in different words. Two prompts are not duplicates merely because both are about the same topic, could use the same photo, or concern the same friend group. Distinct ordinary activities or jokes within one topic are valid. Keep Wild concise and irreverent where the audience permits it; do not demand an elaborate premise or rare photo to demonstrate intensity. Profanity alone does not establish wit, and no card must swear.

These are imaginative photo captions, not factual evidence requests. A joke may playfully project a personality, ownership, a schedule, or an imagined situation onto an ordinary photo. Do not require the image to prove the nonvisual punchline literally. Reject answerability only when players would genuinely need an exact rare photo or cannot reasonably interpret the caption with an ordinary selected-topic photo. For custom topic tags, use the exact selected label for its relevant ordinary category when the group description permits this: a generic pet/dog joke can fit 'Our Dog Milo' without naming Milo or requiring a photograph of that individual. Do not return an empty topic tag merely because a permitted custom name is absent from the caption.
